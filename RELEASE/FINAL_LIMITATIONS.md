# Phase S — Final System Limitations

1. **Scope Restriction**: System scope is strictly limited to Climate Adaptation ONLY for all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu, India.
2. **Quantum Computing**: QAOA is experimental. Quantum Advantage is NOT ESTABLISHED ($Gap = 0.4700$).
3. **Data Delays**: Empirical outcome labels for climate adaptation strategies carry a 1-3 year post-implementation delay.
4. **Spatial Resolution**: Coastal extreme-surge 1km grid downscaling uncertainty is approximately +/- 12%.
5. **Staging HA**: Staging environment runs on single-node PostgreSQL; multi-node HA is deferred.
