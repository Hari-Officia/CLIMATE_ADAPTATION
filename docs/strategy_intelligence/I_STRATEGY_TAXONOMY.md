# Phase I Documentation — Strategy Taxonomy

## 1. Domain Structure (10 Domains)
1. **DOM-DRN**: Drainage & Stormwater
2. **DOM-WTR**: Water Management
3. **DOM-URB**: Urbanization & Land Use
4. **DOM-NBS**: Green / Nature-Based Infrastructure
5. **DOM-BLD**: Built Infrastructure
6. **DOM-GIS**: Terrain / Geometry / GIS Planning
7. **DOM-CIN**: Critical Infrastructure
8. **DOM-EWS**: Early Warning & Preparedness
9. **DOM-HTR**: Heat Resilience
10. **DOM-CST**: Coastal / Marine Resilience
