# Phase I Documentation — Evidence Methodology

## 1. Source Hierarchy
- **Tier 1**: Official Tamil Nadu State Documents (TNSAPCC 2.0, GCC CCAP, TN Wetland Mission, Green TN Mission).
- **Tier 2**: National Agencies (NDMA Guidelines, CGWB Artificial Recharge Reports, IMD Baselines).
- **Tier 3**: International Scientific Assessment Reports (IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 6/18).

## 2. Efficacy Metric Rules
No quantitative percentage reduction claims are assigned to strategy records unless supported by peer-reviewed field study data. Unsubstantiated claims remain `NULL` or `REQUIRES_REVIEW`.
