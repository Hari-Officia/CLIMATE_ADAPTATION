# Phase H Documentation — Scope & System Limitations

## 1. Scope Boundary
- **Adaptation Only**: Strictly excludes climate mitigation actions, renewable energy targets, and carbon offset accounting.
- **Geographic Boundary**: Limited to the 38 administrative districts of Tamil Nadu, India.

## 2. Technical Limitations
- **No Strategy Matching**: Phase H does NOT recommend specific intervention projects (e.g. seawall construction or rainwater harvesting); strategy synthesis occurs in Phase I.
- **No Resource Allocation Optimization**: Phase H does NOT compute optimal budget allocations under constraints; mathematical optimization is performed in Phase J / Phase K / Phase L.
