# Phase H Documentation — Historical & Empirical Validation

## 1. Ground-Truth Alignment
Priority horizon outputs and extracted risk drivers were benchmarked against historical disaster declarations in Tamil Nadu (2015-2023).

## 2. Validation Metrics
- **Coastal Storm Surge / Cyclone Hotspots**: Nagapattinam, Cuddalore, Ramanathapuram correctly identified as `IMMEDIATE` priority with `HIGH_HAZARD_RISK` and `HIGH_CRITICAL_ASSET_EXPOSURE`.
- **Urban Inundation Hotspots**: Chennai and Thiruvallur correctly assigned `IMMEDIATE` priority driven by `HIGH_POPULATION_EXPOSURE` and `HIGH_SENSITIVITY`.
- **Drought-Prone Inland Basins**: Tiruchirappalli and Perambalur correctly flagged for agricultural drought sensitivity drivers.
