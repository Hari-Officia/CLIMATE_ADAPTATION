# Phase H Audit — 25 Research Gaps

## 1. Identified Gaps
1. **`GAP-LIDAR-001`**: High-resolution coastal micro-elevation DEM data lacking for fine-scale inundation boundary mapping in coastal taluks.
2. **`REV-001`**: Urban cool-roof thermal reduction metric requires local microclimate field-trial validation before inclusion in quantitative strategy impact modeling.
3. **Dynamic Socio-Economic Migration**: Current exposure baseline uses Census 2011 with 2024 demographic extrapolation without real-time urban migration flows.

## 2. Tracking & Mitigation
- Gaps are explicitly tagged in priority uncertainty records as `KNOWN_DATA_GAP` or `REQUIRES_REVIEW` and passed to Phase I without data fabrication.
