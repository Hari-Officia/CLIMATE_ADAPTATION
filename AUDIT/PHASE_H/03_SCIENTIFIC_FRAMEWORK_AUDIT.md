## Conceptual Pillar Decomposition
Phase H Adaptation Priority is constructed by combining four independent, verified pillars:
1. **Hazard Risk (Phase E)**: Validated XGBoost classifier probability and risk status.
2. **Population & Built Exposure (Phase F)**: Total population, urban density, critical infrastructure count.
3. **Vulnerability & Sensitivity (Phase G)**: Demographic sensitivity, coastal exposure, elevation context.
4. **Resilience Gap (Phase G)**: Infrastructure preparedness and emergency response capacity.

## Core Governance Rules
- **Decoupled Principles**: Risk ≠ Exposure. Exposure ≠ Vulnerability. Vulnerability ≠ Resilience Gap. Adaptation Priority = Multi-criteria decision profile.
- **No Arbitrary Composite Formula**: No single unverified formula (e.g. `risk * exposure * vulnerability`) is imposed without a registered, versioned methodology (`MTH-PRIORITY-TN-001`).
- **Zero Imputation Policy**: Missing indicators are preserved as `NULL` / status `UNCERTAIN`. No silent zero fill is performed.
